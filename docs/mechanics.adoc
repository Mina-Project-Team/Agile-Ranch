[[game-mechanics]]
=== Dice Roll and Breeding
The player rolls two animal dice. For each non-predator species rolled, the rolled symbols are added to the current herd count for that species, and each full pair grants +1 animal.

=== Market Exchange
Exchange rates: 6 rabbits → 1 sheep; 2 sheep → 1 pig; 3 pigs → 1 cow; 2 cows → 1 horse.
The exchange is immediate, atomic, and unlimited per turn. The current engine supports the listed exchanges only from lower-tier animals to higher-tier animals.

=== Predators and Dogs
- *Fox* (1/12 on the die): takes all rabbits unless the player has a **small dog**.
- *Wolf* (1/12): if the player has a **big dog**, the dog blocks the attack and is removed; otherwise the wolf takes all rabbits, sheep, pigs, and cows while horses remain.
Both effects are resolved after the roll and before breeding.
