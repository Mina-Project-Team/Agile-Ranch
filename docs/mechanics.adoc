= Game Mechanics (Summary)

== Dice Roll and Breeding
The player rolls 2d12. A total of 12 or more grants +1 animal for each full pair of the same species.
If the result is below 12, no breeding occurs.

== Market Exchange
Exchange rates: 6 rabbits → 1 sheep; 2 sheep → 1 pig; 3 pigs → 1 cow; 2 cows → 1 horse.
The exchange is immediate, atomic, and unlimited per turn. Reverse exchange is also supported.

== Predators and Dogs
- *Fox* (1/12 on the die): takes all rabbits unless the player has a **small dog**.
- *Wolf* (1/12): takes all animals except horses and the **big dog**.
Both effects are resolved after the roll and before breeding.
